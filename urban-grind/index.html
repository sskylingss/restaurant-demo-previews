A small independent coffee shop

Built for curious coffee drinkers.

Urban Grind opened in Galway’s Westend to bring new coffees and brew methods to the city. The shelves and grinders keep moving, with blends and single-origin beans ground fresh to take home.

Guest roasters rotate in from across Ireland and Europe—so there is always another cup worth asking about.
