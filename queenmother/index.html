A room layered with Queen West history

A neighbourhood institution

Since 1978,
still evolving.

Queen Mother Café began with 18 seats and grew across three Queen Street addresses—without losing the feeling of a home away from home.

1978Opened on Queen West
1984Garden patio created
1850Roots of this historic site

Long-serving staff and generations of returning guests are part of the story. Lao and Thai staples such as Nam Jeun, Pad Thai and Ping Gai joined the menu around 1982 and remain today.
