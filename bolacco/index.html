A Sun Peaks original

Nearly three decades of mountain stories.

Bolacco Caffé has been family owned and operated since 1996. Inside, the walls read like a travel diary; outside, the mountain does the decorating.

It is the kind of euro-style café where a specialty coffee can turn into breakfast, lunch or a light dinner—and where local hospitality matters as much as the view.

Come for the warm cup. Leave with the next story.
